# Visual Reference Map

The concept images communicate hierarchy, mood and relationships. They are not pixel-perfect specifications and some small generated text may be imperfect. Use the live website copy as the source of truth.

## `visuals/01-home-desktop-concept.png`

Use for:

- asymmetrical desktop hero;
- contrast between blue analysis and amber judgment;
- proof strip with ruled cells;
- condensed display typography;
- quiet technical texture.

Do not copy:

- exact generated text wrapping;
- every tiny diagram label;
- the oversized diagram if it pushes the real CTA too far away.

## `visuals/02-home-mobile-concept.png`

Use for:

- linear mobile order;
- full-width touch-friendly CTAs;
- simplified vertical system story;
- stacked proof metrics.

Important: the diagram comes after the essential content.

## `visuals/03-how-i-work-desktop-concept.png`

Use for:

- four connected engagement stages;
- parallel exploration converging at a human checkpoint;
- outcome callout instead of another generic card.

## `visuals/04-how-i-work-mobile-concept.png`

This is the strongest mobile reference.

Use for:

- one vertical route through the page;
- clear numbered stops;
- large amber decision node;
- process understandable without interaction.

## `visuals/05-case-study-desktop-concept.png`

Use selectively for:

- editorial case header;
- evidence rails and numbered callouts;
- decision log;
- anonymised screenshot framing.

Do not make the entire site look like a classified dossier. Long-form article text should remain calm and conventional.

## `visuals/06-case-study-mobile-concept.png`

Use for:

- compact anchor navigation;
- evidence labels below a full-width visual;
- vertical decision timeline;
- large readable metrics.
